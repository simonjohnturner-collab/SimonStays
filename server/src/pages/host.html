Questions hosts ask

Do I have to leave Airbnb?

No. Most hosts keep their Airbnb and Booking.com listings. SimonStays syncs the calendars so the same night is never sold twice, and brings you extra direct bookings on top.

What does the 10% cover?

Taking the guest’s card payment, holding the dates only once they’ve paid, guest messages and invoices, and your payout. The hosting tools are included.

Do my cleaners need an app?

No. They get a link on WhatsApp and fill in the checklist on their phone, with photos. No password to remember.

Can I still take my own direct bookings?

Yes. Add them to your calendar yourself and send the guest an invoice from SimonStays. There’s no SimonStays fee on those.
